XCSoar firmware update

Update the XCSoar firmware of your device from this page, with a USB cable. Your settings, flights and saved Wi-Fi networks are kept.

This browser cannot talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop computer.

Before you start

  1. Connect the device to this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data, not only power. On a Tab5, use the USB-C port on the side, next to the power button.
  2. In XCSoar, open the menu and press Quit. The screen will say Ready to update (on a LilyGO board, XCSoar has stopped). This makes sure nothing is written to the SD card during the update.
    The screen does not say "Ready to update"

    Firmware older than November 2026 switches off or sleeps after Quit instead. Press the side button to start the device, wait for the "Who is flying?" screen, and update from there without choosing a pilot.

  3. Press Connect below, choose the device in the list the browser shows (often called "USB JTAG/serial debug unit"), and press Connect there.

Installing

Do not unplug the device.

Update finished

The device will restart with the new firmware. Unplug the cable once XCSoar has started.

Something went wrong

Unplug the device, plug it in again and start over. If the update stopped half way, the device will not start until it has been updated, so try again before you unplug it for good.
